Privacy

Short version: the calculator never sends your numbers anywhere, and we only keep your email if you ask for the free sheet.

The calculator

The Debt-Free Date Calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser. The debts, balances and payments you type are not uploaded, logged or shared. Your browser may remember them on your own device so they are still there next time; clearing your browser data removes them.

Your email

If you request the free sheet, your email address is collected by the service that delivers it (Gumroad, or the email tool named on the signup form). We use it to send you the sheet and a few emails about paying off debt and managing money. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. We never sell or rent your address.

Purchases

Payments for the Money Dashboard are handled by Gumroad, which acts as the merchant of record. We never see your card details.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If basic, privacy-friendly visit statistics are added later, this page will say so.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to the email Gumroad sent you with your sheet, and we'll take care of it.
